Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 July 2026

7Bust is a card game. It is not an advertising business and it is not a data business, so this policy is short and it is specific: here is every place the game keeps anything about you, and how long it stays there.

What is kept on your device

Your progress is stored in your browser, or, on CrazyGames and Discord, through that platform's own save feature, so it follows your account instead of one browser. It holds:

  • your chip balance, and the date you last claimed the daily bonus
  • your match history: scores, placings and who won
  • your settings: sound preferences, music volume, and the name you type for online play
  • whether you have accepted the terms and seen the tutorial
  • a random identifier for your browser, used to stop one person taking two seats at the same table

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing site data for the game in your browser settings.

What is kept on our servers

Online tables

While a table is live, the server holds the game state, the display names of the people at it, and the chat messages sent there. Abandoned tables are deleted automatically after six hours. Nothing about a finished match is kept beyond that except as described below.

Chip balances

If you sign in with a CrazyGames or Discord account, your chips are stored on our server so they survive changing device. That record is keyed by a one-way hash of your platform user id: we do not store the id itself, your email, or your real name. The record contains only your balance, the date you last claimed a daily bonus, and a count of rewards claimed today.

Game statistics

The game records aggregate counters, how many matches were played, rounds finished, rewards claimed, to understand whether it is working. Daily counters are kept for about four months. Alongside them, a rolling buffer of the most recent few hundred match results is kept, and those results include the display name of the winner. That is the only place a name is retained after a match ends. Use a nickname if you would rather it were not your own.

What is never collected

  • No email address, password, phone number or real name
  • No raw platform account ids, they are hashed before they are stored
  • No payment details of any kind: the game sells nothing
  • No tracking of you across other websites, and no advertising profile
  • Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers, because none of it would be of use to one

Other services involved

  • CrazyGames: on their site, their SDK handles sign-in, saved progress and adverts under their own privacy policy.
  • Discord: inside a Discord Activity, the game asks Discord only for the identify scope: your user id and display name. It never reads your messages, servers or friends. Discord's handling is covered by their privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts the game and its server, and Upstash stores the data described above. Both keep ordinary server logs, which include IP addresses, for their own operational security.

Children

7Bust is intended for players aged 13 and over, in line with the platforms it appears on. It is not directed at younger children and we do not knowingly keep data from them.

Your choices

Clearing site data removes everything held on your device. To have a server-side chip balance deleted, contact us with the platform you played on and the display name you used, and we will remove it. Because balances are keyed by a hash rather than an account id, we may need you to sign in so the right record can be found.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top will change and the updated version will appear here before the change takes effect.
